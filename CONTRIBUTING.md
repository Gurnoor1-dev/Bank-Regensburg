# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in our virtual banking infrastructure! We welcome the Infinite Flight community's feedback, bug reports, and feature ideas to help us improve the system. 

Because this project is proprietary and legally protected, **we do not accept direct code contributions (Pull Requests).** Please review the guidelines below to see how you can safely contribute to our project.

---

## 🛑 Important Legal Notice
By interacting with this repository, you agree to abide by our project's `LICENSE.md`. 
* **Do not submit Pull Requests containing code.** Any unsolicited Pull Requests with code modifications will be closed without review to protect our proprietary infrastructure.
* **Do not share proprietary or copyrighted material** belonging to other third-party developers or Infinite Flight LLC in your feedback.

---

## 🐛 How to Report Bugs
If you find a glitch, calculation error, or infrastructure breakdown, please let us know so our development team can fix it!

1. **Check Existing Issues:** Before opening a new report, look through our active issues to see if someone else has already reported the problem.
2. **Use the Issue Tracker:** Open a new issue using the "Bug Report" format.
3. **Provide Details:** To help us resolve it quickly, please include:
   * A clear, descriptive title.
   * Steps to reproduce the bug.
   * Expected vs. actual behavior.
   * Screenshots or error logs (ensuring no personal user data or private banking tokens are visible).

## 💡 How to Suggest Features
We love hearing creative ideas on how to expand our banking ecosystem for the Infinite Flight community.

1. **Share the "Why":** Explain what problem the feature solves or how it enhances the virtual airline/pilot banking experience.
2. **Keep it Conceptual:** Describe the idea in plain English. Do not write or include code blocks for how the feature should be implemented.
3. **Be Patient:** Our core development team reviews all suggestions, but implementation depends on our infrastructure roadmap and stability testing.

---

## 🛡️ Community Conduct
We expect all community members, testers, and users to maintain a respectful, constructive, and collaborative tone when opening issues or suggesting features. Insulting behavior or harassment will result in a block from this repository.
